# Deliverables

Keep business deliverables under `deliverables/`. Each document must cite the project sources it used, preserve unresolved facts explicitly, and avoid claiming work or review that has not happened.

| Deliverable | Path | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change impact explanation | `deliverables/songguo-change-impact.md` | Executor |
| Revised work plan | `deliverables/songguo-revised-plan.md` | Executor |
| Source version comparison | `deliverables/songguo-source-versions.md` | Executor |
| Independent review record | `deliverables/sg-independent-review.md` | Independent reviewer only |
| Final delivery and unresolved items | `deliverables/sg-delivery.md` | Executor, after independent review |

The executor should leave the review record absent until the independent reviewer works. The final delivery remains pending until review comments have been received and handled.
